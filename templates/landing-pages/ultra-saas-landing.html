Frequently Asked Questions

How do canary deployments prevent outages?

Canaries test fresh code on 1% of live requests. If error metrics or latency spike by more than 0.1%, traffic is automatically shifted back in under 50ms without human intervention.

Can I connect my existing AWS or GCP infrastructure?

Yes. Hyperplane sits as a distributed edge caching and routing layer in front of your existing VPCs via WireGuard or PrivateLink.
